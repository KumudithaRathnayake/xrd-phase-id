# Example data

`cpd-1a.txt`: powder XRD scan of sample 1a from the International Union of Crystallography Commission on Powder Diffraction (IUCr CPD) round robin on quantitative phase analysis, a three-phase mixture of corundum (Al2O3), fluorite (CaF2) and zincite (ZnO). Lab Cu Kα data (Kα1 + Kα2), 5–150° 2θ in 0.02° steps; two columns: 2θ (°), counts.

Reference: I. C. Madsen, N. V. Y. Scarlett, L. M. D. Cranswick and T. Lwin, "Outcomes of the International Union of Crystallography Commission on Powder Diffraction Round Robin on Quantitative Phase Analysis: samples 1a to 1h", *J. Appl. Cryst.* 34, 409–426 (2001).
